Understanding Fake Reviews

🚩 Signs of Fake Reviews

  • Excessive use of superlatives (BEST, AMAZING, PERFECT)
  • Too many exclamation marks (!!!)
  • Generic praise without specific details
  • Overly promotional language
  • Perfect grammar (suspiciously clean)
  • Extreme ratings with simple explanations

✅ Signs of Real Reviews

  • Balanced feedback with pros and cons
  • Specific details about product usage
  • Natural language patterns and minor errors
  • Context about purchase reason or situation
  • Moderate tone without excessive enthusiasm
  • Constructive criticism or suggestions
